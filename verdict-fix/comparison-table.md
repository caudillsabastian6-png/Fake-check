# Real vs fake — how each is made, and what can be seen from where

Ratings: **A** = visible in an ordinary phone photo · **B** = needs a scanner (≥ ~400 dpi) or a phone macro/loupe shot · **C** = needs the physical card (hands, scale, light, edge).
"House" = what the Rosette record says it has measured (deepdive.md §4.5 / §5).

| # | Tell | Genuine (how it's made) | Typical fake (how it's made) | A | B | C | House status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Print screen / rosette | Offset litho, separate C/M/Y/K plates at fixed angles → regular dot rosette in colour areas | Inkjet = random FM spray; laser = toner scatter, weak or no rosette; scan-rebuilt offset fakes copy a rosette *of a rosette* (moiré) | – | ✔ | – | P-core (dots inside the back "P") is the one validated check — scanner only, one fake class |
| 2 | Solid black / solid colour layers | Black text and some fills from a dedicated plate → crisp, dot-free | Black built from CMYK dots, halos, doubled edges; rosettes where solid should be | weak | ✔ | – | Measured inside back "P" (T1). Front border version 7.3% FF (over bar), back border dead |
| 3 | Back blue / dappling | Same English back since 1999; several blue shades above-right of the ball; navy frame distinct from swirl | One flat blue, pale or purple; frame bleeds into swirl | ✔ (white-balance sensitive) | ✔ | – | Back-lane dappling AUC 1.0 on pair-sheet family only |
| 4 | Small text / copyright line | Sharp micro text | Blurred or broken (scan-rebuilt) | weak | ✔ | – | Lane D: strong on proxy family, blind on file-built fakes |
| 5 | Fonts, kerning, energy symbols | Licensed fonts, exact layout | "Close enough" fonts, symbols 2–5% off, line breaks shift | ✔ (needs same-card reference) | ✔ | – | Lane D (T5), family-bound |
| 6 | Text facts (é, HP, typos, set number) | Proofread, catalogued | Typos, missing é, impossible HP, numbers not in set | ✔ | – | – | T13, cheap, catches only non-copies |
| 7 | Holo pattern / masking | Foil laminated on stock *before* printing; opaque white layer masks where foil hides; era pattern (Starlight→Cosmos→…→Mirage) | Generic rainbow over whole card, wrong era pattern, foil over text boxes | partial (one still) | – | tilt video ✔ | T7 qualitative; T9 tilt video proposed, unmeasured |
| 8 | Texture on full arts | Etched texture following the art, applied as separate layer | Smooth, or printed fake texture, or generic grid | – | raking light | ✔ fingertip | T10 proposed, unmeasured |
| 9 | Core layer | Two paper plies glued with dark (black/blue-grey) core; blocks light | Single ply, white throughout; better fakes add a dark layer | – | edge macro | ✔ edge, light test | T11, edge metric 51% catch @4% FF (pair-sheet) |
| 10 | Light test | Faint even glow | Bright glow (cheap fakes); super-fakes pass | – | – | ✔ (unreliable, language-dependent) | Not a tell (TCGplayer & Bulbapedia both call it unreliable) |
| 11 | Size, corners, cut | 63×88 mm, cut square then corner-rounded in a separate step | Size drift, straight guillotine cuts, uneven corners | weak | ✔ | ✔ | H7 / T12; edge/cut lane at chance in loop form |
| 12 | Centering | Off-centre is common on real cards | Anything | ✔ | – | – | Descriptor only, never a tell |
| 13 | Weight / thickness | ~1.7–2.0 g by era/finish | Often lighter, sometimes heavier (sticker / glued-on fronts nearly double thickness) | – | – | ✔ | Hands-only (H6) |
| 14 | Colour / darkness | Calibrated press (spectrophotometers on press) | Washed out or too dark | ✔ but rewritten by phone AWB | ✔ scanner | – | Cuts three ways; absolute thresholds fail across domains |
| 15 | Price / seller words | — | "proxy/custom/orica", far below market | listing | – | – | Never a photo verdict input (T16) |
