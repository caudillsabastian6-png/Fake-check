# How to shoot a matched pair (one page)

For Sabastian and Potion, starting with the Evolving Skies fake box.

**The goal:** one real card and one fake of the SAME card, photographed the SAME way, so the only difference in the photos is the card itself, not the camera.

## Before you start
- Pick one fake from the box. Find a real copy of that same card (same name, same number). If you don't own one, note it and move on to the next fake.
- Write the card number and "REAL" or "FAKE" on a sticky note. Never on the card.
- One table, one lamp. Turn off the overhead light and close blinds, so the light doesn't change halfway through.
- Plain dark background (black t-shirt or mouse pad). No carpet. No patterned table.
- Phone flash OFF. Wipe the lens.

## The shots (do every shot for BOTH cards before you move anything)
1. **Both fronts in one photo.** Real on the LEFT, fake on the RIGHT, side by side, not touching. Phone straight above, about 30 cm (one foot) up. Both cards fill most of the photo.
2. **Both backs in one photo.** Flip both in place. Real stays on the LEFT.
3. **Each front alone.** Same height, same spot on the table. Card fills the frame.
4. **Each back alone.** Same as 3.
5. **Back-logo close-up of each card.** Get the phone 3–5 cm (about 2 inches) from the yellow "P" at the top of the back. If the phone can't focus that close, back off a little and zoom 2x instead. Tap the screen on the logo to focus. Retake if it's blurry.
6. **Edge of each card.** Stand the card up or hold it at the table edge, and shoot the long edge straight on, close up. You're looking for the thin dark line in the middle.
7. **Holo only:** a 3-second video of each card tilting slowly left to right under the lamp.
8. **Scanner (if you have one):** put both cards on the glass together, real left, fake right, at 600 dpi or higher. One scan for fronts, one for backs.

## Rules that matter
- **Same session.** Real and fake get shot in the same sitting, same light, same phone, same distance. Never shoot the real one today and the fake tomorrow.
- **Same settings.** Don't change zoom, light or height between the real and the fake shot of the same kind.
- **Name the files right away**, like `EVS-215-real-front.jpg`, `EVS-215-fake-front.jpg`, `EVS-215-pair-backs.jpg`. Card number first.
- **Two real copies of the same card are gold.** If you have them, shoot them as a "real vs real" pair too. That's how we learn what normal variation looks like.
- **Keep some fakes sealed.** Before shooting, set aside a few fakes that nobody photographs or measures. Latch decides which ones. They're the blind test later.
- Light test: if you do it, write down what you saw. It's a note, not a score.

## Hand-off
Put every photo for one card in a folder named by the card number, with a note saying which file is real and which is fake and who said so. Send the folder to Potion. Don't add these to any training pile until Latch signs off.
